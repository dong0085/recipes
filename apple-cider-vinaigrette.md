# Apple Cider Vinaigrette

Tags: dressing, vegetarian, quick, no-cook, make-ahead
Serves: 4
Time: 5 min

## Ingredients

- 45 ml olive oil, 3 tbsp
- 15 ml apple cider vinegar, 1 tbsp
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 pinch salt and pepper

## Steps

1. Shake everything in a jar with the lid on until it turns creamy.

## Notes

- Keeps in the fridge for 5 days. Shake again before using.
- Lemon juice works in place of the vinegar.
