# Avocado Hummus

## Ingredients

- 1 can chickpeas, 400 g / 15 oz, drained (about 240 g)
- 1½ avocados, or 2 small
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1–2 cloves garlic
- 8 g tahini, about 2 tsp
- 25 ml olive oil, about 1½ tbsp
- 3 g salt
- 3 g pepper

## Steps

1. Prepare the ingredients, then put everything in a food processor or blender.
2. Blend until smooth and silky. Scrape down the sides a few times, and add a splash of water if it's too thick.

No blender? Mince the garlic, then mash everything with a fork or potato masher. It comes out chunkier, which is good too.

## Notes

Serve with toast, salad, or tortilla chips.
