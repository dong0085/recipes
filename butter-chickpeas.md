# Butter Chickpeas

Spices:
- 4 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 4 bay leaves

1. Butter, then onions.
2. Garlic and ginger.
3. Add the spices and bay leaves.
4. Tomatoes, then simmer 15 min.
5. Chickpeas.
6. Stir in 4 tbsp cream.
7. Finish with fresh cilantro. Remove bay leaves before serving.
