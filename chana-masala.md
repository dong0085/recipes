# Chana Masala

Tags: indian, main, vegetarian, one-pot

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp oil or ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2 onions, finely chopped, about 300 g
- 4 cloves garlic, grated
- 2.5 cm / 1 in ginger, grated
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder, or ½ tsp cayenne plus 1 tsp paprika
- 1 can diced tomatoes, 400 g / 14 oz
- 2 cans chickpeas, 400 g / 15 oz each, drained
- 250 ml water, 1 cup
- 1½ tsp salt, plus more to taste
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp amchur (dried mango powder), or 1–2 tsp lemon juice
- A handful of cilantro, chopped

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and fry for 30 seconds, until they sizzle and darken a shade.
2. Add the onions and a pinch of salt. Cook for 12–15 minutes, stirring often, until deep golden brown.
3. Add the garlic and ginger and cook for 1 minute.
4. Turn the heat to low. Add the coriander, ground cumin, turmeric, and chili powder with a splash of water, and stir for 30 seconds.
5. Add the tomatoes. Cook for 8–10 minutes, until they break down into a thick paste and the oil starts to separate at the edges.
6. Add the chickpeas, water, and salt. Simmer for 15–20 minutes. Mash a handful of chickpeas against the side of the pot to thicken the sauce.
7. Stir in the garam masala and amchur or lemon. Taste and add salt until the flavour is bright. Top with cilantro.

## Notes

- Use Indian chili powder or cayenne. American "chili powder" is a Tex-Mex blend with oregano and cumin, and it makes this taste like chili con carne.
- Most of the flavour comes from the long-cooked onions and tomatoes in steps 2 and 5, so give them the full time.
- Serve with rice, naan, or roti.
