# Chili

1. Cook onion, carrot, bell pepper, celery, and salt for 7 min.
2. Add the spices:
   - 4 cloves garlic
   - 2 tbsp chili powder
   - 2 tsp cumin
   - 1 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
   - 1 tsp oregano
3. Add tomatoes, beans, veggie broth, and a bay leaf. Simmer 30 min.
4. Finish with cilantro, vinegar, and salt.
