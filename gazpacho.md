# Gazpacho

Tags: soup, vegetarian, make-ahead, no-cook, cold
Serves: 4
Time: 20 min, plus 2 hr chilling

## Ingredients

- 1 kg / 2¼ lb ripe tomatoes, cored and roughly chopped
- 1 cucumber, about 300 g, peeled and roughly chopped
- 1 green bell pepper, seeded and roughly chopped
- ¼ small red onion, about 40 g
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 slice day-old white bread, about 40 g, crust removed
- 2 tbsp sherry vinegar, plus more to taste
- 1½ tsp salt, plus more to taste
- 120 ml / ½ cup extra virgin olive oil
- Cold water, as needed

To serve:
- Finely diced cucumber, pepper, and red onion
- Croutons
- Olive oil, for drizzling

## Steps

1. Put the tomatoes, cucumber, pepper, onion, garlic, bread, vinegar, and salt in a large bowl. Toss and leave for 15 minutes, so the salt draws out the juices and the bread softens.
2. Blend everything until very smooth, about 2 minutes.
3. With the blender running, pour in the olive oil in a thin stream. The soup turns pale orange and creamy.
4. Press it through a fine sieve for a silky texture, or leave it as is for a more rustic one.
5. Thin with cold water if it is thicker than heavy cream. Taste and add vinegar or salt until it is bright.
6. Chill for at least 2 hours. Taste again before serving, since cold dulls the seasoning.
7. Serve in bowls or glasses, topped with the diced vegetables, croutons, and a drizzle of oil.

## Notes

- Make this in summer, when tomatoes are at their best. It is only as good as the tomatoes.
- It keeps for 2 days in the fridge and tastes better on the second day.
