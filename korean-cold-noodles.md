# Korean Cold Noodles

Prep:
- Noodles, cooked and cooled
- Shredded cabbage
- Carrots, cut into matchsticks
- Cucumber, cut into matchsticks
- Edamame

Sauce:
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1.5 tbsp gochujang
- Rice vinegar
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Sesame seeds
- 1.5 tbsp brown sugar

Garnish:
- Green onions, sliced

The handwritten note does not specify quantities for the noodles, vegetables, edamame, rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, sesame seeds, or green onions. Adjust these to taste.

Suggested assembly:
1. Cook the noodles according to the package directions, rinse under cold water, and drain well.
2. Cook the edamame according to the package directions, cool, and shell if needed.
3. Shred the cabbage and cut the carrots and cucumber into matchsticks.
4. Mix the sauce ingredients, adjusting the unmeasured ingredients to taste.
5. Toss the cooled noodles, vegetables, and edamame with the sauce.
6. Garnish with green onions and serve cold.
