# Lentil Soup

1. Cook onion, celery, carrots, and salt in olive oil for 6–8 min, until soft.
2. Add the spices and stir for 1 min:
   - 3 cloves garlic
   - 1 tsp cumin
   - 1 tsp coriander
   - 1 tsp oregano
3. Add 1 1/2 cups brown or green lentils, 1 diced sweet potato, 6 cups veggie broth, and 1 can crushed tomatoes. Simmer 30 min, until the lentils are soft.
4. Optional: blend about a third of the soup and stir it back in.
5. Add greens (kale 5 min, spinach 1 min).
6. Finish with 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar and salt.

Italian version: swap the cumin, coriander, and oregano for basil, thyme, and rosemary.
