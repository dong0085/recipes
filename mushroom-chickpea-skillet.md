# Mushroom and Chickpea Skillet

Tags: main, vegetarian, quick, one-pot
Serves: 3
Time: 30 min

Whole mushrooms seared until browned, with creamy chickpeas, tender zucchini, and sweet-sour sun-dried tomatoes. Herbs, soy sauce, and lemon juice season it, so it tastes savoury and fresh at the same time.

## Ingredients

- 2 tbsp olive oil, or oil from the sun-dried tomato jar
- 400 g / 14 oz small cremini or button mushrooms, whole
- 1 onion, chopped, about 150 g
- ½ tsp salt, plus more to taste
- 1 zucchini, about 250 g, cut into chunks
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 60 g / ½ cup oil-packed sun-dried tomatoes, chopped
- 1 can chickpeas, 400 g / 15 oz, drained, or 250 g / 1½ cups cooked
- 1 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Black pepper, a few grinds
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2–4 tbsp water, as needed
- 2 large handfuls leafy greens, such as spinach or kale, about 100 g
- A handful of cilantro, chopped
- Juice of ½ lemon

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms in one layer and leave them alone for 3–4 minutes, until browned underneath. Turn them and brown for 3–4 minutes more.
2. Add the onion and the salt. Cook for 4–5 minutes, until the onion is soft.
3. Add the zucchini and cook for 3–4 minutes, until it starts to soften.
4. Add the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes and cook for 1 minute.
5. Add the chickpeas, paprika, coriander, oregano, and black pepper. Fry for 2 minutes, stirring, so the spices toast.
6. Add the soy sauce and a splash of water if the pan looks dry. Stir until everything is coated in the sauce.
7. Add the greens and stir for 1–2 minutes, until wilted.
8. Take the pan off the heat. Stir in the cilantro, then the lemon juice. Taste and add salt if needed.

## Notes

- Don't crowd the mushrooms or stir them early. The browning in step 1 is where most of the flavour comes from. Use two batches if they don't fit in one layer.
- Ground coriander is the dried seed of the cilantro plant. It tastes warm and lightly citrusy, and parsley can't replace it. If you don't have it, use ½ tsp ground cumin or just leave it out.
- The video doesn't say when to add the soy sauce. Step 6 is a good place, since it seasons the sauce that coats everything.
- Serve with rice, bread, or on its own.
