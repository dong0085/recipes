# Salads

Tags: side, vegetarian, quick, no-cook, make-ahead

Each dressing makes enough for one big salad, about 4 servings.

## Dressings

Shake everything in a jar with the lid on. Both keep in the fridge for 5 days.

### Apple Cider Vinaigrette

- 45 ml olive oil, 3 tbsp
- 15 ml apple cider vinegar, 1 tbsp
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 pinch salt and pepper

Lemon juice works in place of the vinegar.

### Tahini Lemon Dressing

- 30 g tahini, 2 tbsp
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 small clove garlic, grated
- 3–4 tbsp cold water
- ½ tsp salt

Whisk the tahini and lemon first. It will thicken and look split; keep adding water a spoonful at a time until it is smooth and pourable.

## Salads

### Purple Cabbage, Apple, and Chickpea Salad

With apple cider vinaigrette.

- ¼ head purple cabbage, about 300 g / 4 cups shredded
- 1 crisp apple, such as Honeycrisp or Gala
- 1 can chickpeas, 400 g / 15 oz, drained and rinsed
- A handful of parsley, chopped
- 2 tbsp toasted sunflower seeds or walnuts, optional

1. Make the dressing in a large bowl.
2. Shred the cabbage as thinly as you can. Add it to the bowl and toss well, so the vinegar starts to soften it.
3. Cut the apple into matchsticks and toss it in right away, so it stays white.
4. Add the chickpeas and parsley, toss again, and rest for 10 minutes. Top with the seeds or walnuts.

Keeps in the fridge for 2 days. To make ahead, add the apple and seeds just before serving.
