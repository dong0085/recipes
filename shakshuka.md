# Shakshuka

Tags: middle-eastern, main, vegetarian, one-pot
Serves: 4
Time: 40 min

## Ingredients

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, thinly sliced, about 150 g
- 2 red bell peppers, thinly sliced, about 300 g
- 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
- 4 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp sweet paprika
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- ¼–½ tsp chili flakes, or 1 tsp harissa
- 1 can whole tomatoes, 800 g / 28 oz, crushed by hand
- 1 tsp sugar, if the tomatoes are sharp
- 6 eggs
- 100 g / 3½ oz feta, crumbled
- A handful of parsley or cilantro, chopped
- Crusty bread or pita, to serve

## Steps

1. Heat the oil in a large lidded skillet, about 30 cm / 12 in, over medium heat. Add the onion, peppers, and a pinch of the salt. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft and starting to brown at the edges.
2. Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
3. Add the cumin, both paprikas, and the chili flakes. Stir for 30 seconds, until fragrant.
4. Add the tomatoes and the rest of the salt. Simmer for 10–15 minutes, until the sauce is thick enough that a spoon dragged through it leaves a path. Taste and add the sugar or more salt if needed.
5. Turn the heat to low. Make 6 wells in the sauce with the back of a spoon and crack an egg into each.
6. Cover and cook for 5–8 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks are still runny.
7. Scatter the feta and herbs over the top. Serve straight from the pan with bread.

## Notes

- Check the eggs early. The yolks keep cooking in the hot pan after it comes off the heat.
- The sauce through step 4 keeps for 3 days in the fridge. Reheat it until bubbling before adding the eggs.
- For a vegan version, leave out the eggs and feta and add a drained can of chickpeas in step 4.
