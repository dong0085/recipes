# Tahini Lemon Dressing

Tags: dressing, vegetarian, quick, no-cook, make-ahead
Serves: 4
Time: 5 min

## Ingredients

- 30 g tahini, 2 tbsp
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 small clove garlic, grated
- 3–4 tbsp cold water
- ½ tsp salt

## Steps

1. Whisk the tahini and lemon juice. It will thicken and look split.
2. Add the water a spoonful at a time, whisking, until smooth and pourable. Stir in the garlic and salt.

## Notes

- Keeps in the fridge for 5 days. It thickens as it sits, so loosen it with a little water.
- Good on roasted vegetables, grain bowls, and kale.
